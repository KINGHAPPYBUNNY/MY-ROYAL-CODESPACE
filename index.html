🧭 Wikipedia Edit Statistics with Facebook Share, Like & Comments

📌 Project Objectives

  • To visualize key editing metrics such as top editors, most edited articles, and edit frequency.
  • To provide detailed insights into the edit history and contributor statistics of specific Wikipedia articles.
  • To create profiles for top Wikipedia editors, showcasing their contributions and areas of expertise.
  • To encourage community engagement by facilitating discussions between Wikipedia editors and Facebook users.
